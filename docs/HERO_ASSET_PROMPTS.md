# Prompts des assets du hero

Les trois assets ci-dessous ont ete generes avec l'outil ImageGen integre. Les fonds blanc et noir sont volontaires : l'integration web utilise respectivement les modes de fusion CSS `multiply` et `screen`.

## Scene Goyave

Fichier : `/public/media/mpm/hero/assets/goyave-scene-v1.png`

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: layered website hero asset to be composited with multiply blend mode
Primary request: Photorealistic fresh pink guava fruit and tropical foliage surrounding an empty center for a tall beverage can.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly uniform pure white (#FFFFFF), absolutely no checkerboard, no gradient, no shadow cast on the background
Subject: whole green guava and two juicy pink guava halves with condensation in the two lower corners, glossy guava leaves, one elegant palm frond entering from upper right, thin clear water splash curving low behind the empty center
Style/medium: premium commercial beverage photography, realistic textures, crisp edges
Composition/framing: wide landscape 3:2; isolated edge clusters; very generous empty center; nothing crosses the central vertical product area
Lighting/mood: bright tropical daylight, fresh and sophisticated
Constraints: pure white background; no can, bottle, package, logo, text, watermark, frame or checkerboard
Avoid: illustration, fake labels, beige or gray background, clutter, central obstruction
```

## Feuille de palmier

Fichier : `/public/media/mpm/hero/assets/palm-frond-v1.png`

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: reusable botanical depth layer for tropical beverage website heroes, composed with multiply blend mode
Primary request: A single elegant tropical palm frond with long natural green leaflets, isolated and extending diagonally from the upper right toward the lower left.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly uniform pure white (#FFFFFF), absolutely no checkerboard, gradient, texture or cast shadow
Subject: one realistic palm frond only, fresh glossy leaves with a few subtle water droplets
Style/medium: premium commercial botanical photography, crisp realistic detail
Composition/framing: wide landscape 3:2, stem begins outside the upper-right edge, frond occupies the right half and reaches toward center, ample empty white area
Lighting/mood: clean tropical daylight, refined and natural
Constraints: pure white background; exactly one frond; no fruit, no can, no bottle, no package, no text, no logo, no watermark
Avoid: illustration, checkerboard, beige or gray background, extra plants, frames
```

## Energie Triplex

Fichier : `/public/media/mpm/hero/assets/triplex-energy-v1.png`

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: layered website hero asset to be composited with screen blend mode
Primary request: Photorealistic premium energy-drink atmosphere framing an empty center for a beverage can.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly uniform pure black (#000000), no gradient and no visible set
Subject: sweeping arc of amber carbonated drink with fine bubbles along the lower edge, crystal-clear ice cubes concentrated in the two lower corners, sparse condensation droplets, restrained red-orange light streaks entering from the side edges
Style/medium: high-end commercial beverage photography, sharp realistic liquid and ice, not illustration
Composition/framing: wide landscape 3:2; elements around the edges and lower third; large unobstructed empty center and upper center
Lighting/mood: dramatic rim light, energetic but premium
Color palette: amber gold, clear ice, red-orange highlights
Constraints: pure black background; no can, bottle, glass, hand, logo, text, watermark, flames, lightning symbol or frame
Avoid: checkerboard, gray background, cartoon effects, clutter, central obstruction
```
